# PROJECT SUMMARY / ABSTRACT (SF424 Section 7)

**Project Title:** Ascorbate-Derived Chemotype Library and SAR Enrichment for
alpha9alpha10 Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor Positive Allosteric Modulators

**Project period:** 09/01/2027 - 08/31/2030 (3 years, 3 budget periods)

**Project Summary (public abstract):**
Nicotinic acetylcholine receptors containing alpha9alpha10 subunits are a
validated, yet chemically untouched, target for chronic pain and hearing-related
disorders. No positive allosteric modulator (PAM) of alpha9alpha10 has advanced
into the clinic, in part because the known agonist/antagonist chemistry does not
transfer. We will build a chemically diverse library of alpha9alpha10 PAM
chemotypes derived from the L-ascorbic acid (vitamin C) pharmacophore — the
only validated PAM chemotype for this target and a selective alpha9alpha10
potentiator whose L- and D-forms are approximately equipotent — and use it to
enrich existing structure-activity relationships (SAR) for future
drug-discovery and preclinical studies. Starting from a curated experimental dataset (30 compounds, 7 validated
PAM actives, 24 sharing the ascorbate gamma-lactone enediol core) and a
141-molecule computational candidate portfolio, we will: (Aim 1) curate a
drug-like, chemically diverse library of Bemis-Murcko-distinct chemotypes with
pre-registered filters (PAINS/BRENK removal, Lipinski gates, stereochemical
probes), characterize ascorbate-core sub-series around the reference L-ascorbate
and its equally potent D-form (3-O-alkoxy high-potency arm, 5,6-acetonides,
6-substituted variants), and deliver a structure-guided enrichment layer pending
Alphafold3 binding-model validation; (Aim 2) run a pre-registered, two-armed
closed-loop experiment in which model-ranked and diversity-randomized compounds
are tested in parallel by two-electrode voltage-clamp electrophysiology
(co-PI laboratory) on Xenopus oocytes; and (Aim 3) convert each round's
confirmed hits into an enriched, AI-ready SAR resource (matched-molecular-pair
rules, ascorbate sub-series summaries, versioned predictive models) validated
against published nicotinic-receptor data. Success criteria (enrichment factor
>=2.0, BH-FDR alpha=0.10, dose-response confirmation) are pre-registered before
any experiment. This project delivers the first experimentally enumerated
ascorbate-derived alpha9alpha10 PAM chemotype library and a reusable,
adaptive SAR-enrichment platform applicable to any ion-channel target.

**Relevance statement (for the public):**
This project identifies and tests novel chemical families based on vitamin C
(ascorbic acid) that safely modulate a receptor implicated in chronic pain and
hearing loss, and converts each round of results into shared chemical knowledge
that speeds up future drug discovery.
