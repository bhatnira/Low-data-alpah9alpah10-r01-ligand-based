# VERTEBRATE ANIMALS (PHS 398, section 6 / SF424 criterion)

**Is a vertebrate animal component involved?** Yes (Xenopus laevis, oocyte
source), pending institutional classification. We describe the arrangement
honestly to avoid audit issues at resubmission.

## What is used
- Isolated **Xenopus oocytes** purchased from a commercial supplier used for
  two-electrode voltage-clamp recording of heterologously expressed
  alpha9alpha10 nAChRs. Oocytes arrive as isolated cells; the supplier maintains
  the animals under its own oversight.
- No live animals are housed, manipulated, or transported by the awardee
  institution in this project.

## Justification
Xenopus oocytes are the standard, licensed platform for this heteromeric
nAChR combination and are required for quantitative TEVC measurement of
potentiation. No alternative in vitro system reproduces the alpha9alpha10
combination with equivalent fidelity. Sample sizes are minimal by design
(3 oocytes x ~15 compounds/round x 3 rounds, plus confirmation
dose-response replicates); pooling rounds is the statistical strategy, not
increasing animals.

## Species and number
- Species: Xenopus laevis (supplier; oocyte donor).
- Oocytes: ~500 total across the 3-year period, well below thresholds
  requiring USDA registration; supplier holds its own assurance.

## Veterinary care / procurement
- Oocytes sourced from a commercial supplier with institutional oversight and
  animal-welfare assurance documentation on file; veterinary care is the
  supplier's responsibility and is documented in procurement records.

## Assurance
- If the receiving institution classifies oocyte procurement as regulated
  vertebrate use, an IACUC protocol (or an official exemption letter) will be
  obtained and its number included in the final application before any
  purchase. We commit to the highest welfare standards and to donor
  minimization in every round design.

## Refinement / reduction / replacement
- Refinement: blinding and staged rounds minimize oocyte use.
- Reduction: within-round replicate plan is statistically justified in the
  pre-registration; power is evaluated on pooled rounds.
- Replacement: heterologous expression replaces whole-animal experiments.
