# 2. SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE

Develop an AI-augmented, experimentally grounded workflow that:

1. learns the experimentally observed α9α10 PAM SAR;
2. identifies reproducible ligand-based activity patterns;
3. quantifies model uncertainty and applicability domain;
4. uses **stereochemistry-aware Mol2Mol generation as the primary chemical prior**;
5. uses reinforcement learning for controlled optimization;
6. performs constrained and broader chemical exploration;
7. prioritizes compounds for experimental synthesis/testing;
8. evaluates plausible cochlear delivery;
9. evaluates systemic ADME;
10. evaluates systemic toxicity;
11. evaluates local cochlear/hair-cell safety;
12. evaluates pharmacological selectivity;
13. explicitly identifies uncertainty and information gaps;
14. produces a traceable final candidate portfolio;
15. evaluates success prospectively through experimental testing.

The goal is **not** to maximize retrospective ML metrics.

The ultimate criterion is:

> Does the computational workflow improve prospective medicinal-chemistry decision-making and experimental enrichment?

# 87. FINAL PRINCIPLE

The computational system must behave as a **scientific decision-support engine**, not as an autonomous truth generator.

The central philosophy is:

> Experimental α9α10 PAM SAR provides the ground truth. A stereochemistry-aware Mol2Mol chemical prior provides local, experimentally anchored chemical exploration. REINVENT4 reinforcement learning optimizes within validated ligand-based SAR constraints, while LibInvent and limited scaffold hopping provide controlled broader exploration. Independent downstream analyses evaluate synthesis, cochlear delivery, ADME, systemic toxicity, local cochlear safety, diversity, information gain, and finally pharmacological selectivity. All predictions retain uncertainty and provenance, and every final candidate must remain traceable to the experimental evidence that motivated its generation.

The system is successful only if it produces **reproducible, experimentally testable, scientifically interpretable candidate hypotheses** and demonstrates prospective value through experimental validation.
