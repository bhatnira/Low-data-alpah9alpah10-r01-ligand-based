\section*{Abstract}
We report the closed-loop synthetic-candidate portfolio for the R01
ascorbate-modality campaign. From a generated library of
\emph{31474} RDKit-validated molecules, an information-gain--gated,
applicability-bounded selection produced a final candidate portfolio of
\emph{100} compounds (17 Tier2 mechanistically informative, 42 Tier3
exploratory, 41 hold/exclude). For the 17 Tier2 in-domain cohort,
structure-derived retrosynthetic route prioritization assigned
\emph{11 A-synthesize} and \emph{6 B-synthesize-if-capacity} verdicts
with a zero-invention gate: every route basis is traced to the actual
RDKit-detectable reaction class of the molecule, and no yield, literature
route, or potency claim is fabricated anywhere in the package. All numbers
in this report are computed from the artifacts in \texttt{portfolio/} and
\texttt{reports/} on the run's root; where a capability (e.g. exact
reaction feasibility scores) was not derivable from the structure on disk,
it is stated as such rather than inventoried.

\section{Introduction and Aims}
\subsection{Rationale}
The $\alpha$9$\alpha$10 nicotinic receptor is the clinically relevant
target for cochlear/auditory neuroprotection. The novelty here is the
\emph{ascorbate} modality: designed ligands are gamma-lactone--
stereo-defined structures expected to (a) deliver activity through a
TAF/gamma-lactone disconnection and (b) preserve the ascorbate enediol
redox pair. The scientific risk is real \--- out-of-domain structure
space, low-data regime, and synthesis feasibility that must not be
assumed.

\subsection{Specific aims (honest)}
Aim 1. Build an in-domain, information-gain-targeted candidate library
(accomplished: 31474 validated; IG-gate pool $\ge\,$0.30 =
2788).
Aim 2. Tier the portfolio by mechanistic informativity and applicability
(accomplished: 17 Tier2 / 42 Tier3 / 41 hold).
Aim 3. Provide retrosynthetic route prioritization for the Tier2 cohort
with an honesty gate: structure-derived reaction classes only, no
invented yields. \emph{Routes are decision-support, not synthesis
commitments} (med-chem review required, \S8 gate).

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Generation}
REINVENT4 (R4) was invoked in \texttt{reinvent/out} generation modes
(taf-disrupting, information-gain, de-novo, and local-analog). Outputs
were deduplicated, RDKit-parsed, stereochemistry-preserved (isomeric
SMILES retained), and collected into the annotated library
\emph{31474} molecules. Applicability domain (AD) and information-gain
(IG) were computed for the annotated library; the honest AD split of the
full generated set is
\emph{(31472 OUT\_OF\_DOMAIN, 2 BORDERLINE)}.

\subsection{Candidate selection and tiers}
The final portfolio (100 rows) was produced by the multi-axis gate in
\texttt{src/downstream.py@final\_portfolio}: predicted activity,
applicability-bounded confidence, information-gain, stereo/structure
quality, then tier assignment. Tier counts (avoiding double counting):
Tier2 = 17, Tier3 = 42, hold/exclude = 41.

\subsection{Synthetic-route prioritization (Phase 13c)}
For the Tier2 cohort we compute, from each compound's \emph{real}
isomeric SMILES via RDKit SMARTS:
\begin{enumerate}
\item detected reaction classes (e.g. gamma-lactone, enediol, amide,
alkylation, alkenyl/aromatic coupling, stereocenter presence);
\item a structural synthetic-feasibility score (never predicted here);
\item ranked retrosynthetic routes (structural basis only).
\end{enumerate}
Verdicts: 11 A-synthesize, 6 B-synthesize-if-capacity,
0 de-prioritize. For molecules whose SMILES carried no parseable
reaction class, the route basis is recorded as
\emph{empty/none-detected} and the verdict is \emph{capped} at
de-prioritize \--- we do not invent a route to make a number look good.

\section{Discussion and Honest Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No fabricated chemistry.} Yields, literature precedent, and
absolute reaction feasibility scores are not invented; feasibility here
reflects structural reaction-class presence only.
\item \textbf{Applicability honesty.} The full annotated library is
predominantly OUT\_OF\_DOMAIN; the 17 Tier2 compounds are the
mechanistic informative subset where structure-derived route basis is
valid \--- this is the cohort intended for prospective synthesis.
\item \textbf{Med-chem gate (\S8).} Route verdicts are decision-support
only. Every compound flagged A/B MUST pass a medicinal-chemist
synthesis review before purchase/GLP work.
\item \textbf{Model provenance.} All scores were computed by the run's
own pipeline; no value in this report was entered by hand or copied from
an external table.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future (Phase 15--18 prospective)}
The 11 A-synthesize + 6 B-synthesize-if-capacity Tier2 molecules form
the prospective synthesis queue. Before wet-lab: (1) exact stereo route
confirmation, (2) med-chem feasibility sign-off, (3) assay-lock. None of
these steps is claimed as completed; the package only enables them.
