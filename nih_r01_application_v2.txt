SECTION 1: PROJECT SUMMARY/ABSTRACT
=======================================================================

Project Title:
Development of High-Potency, Selective Ascorbate-Derived alpha9alpha10
Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor Positive Allosteric Modulators through an
Integrated Medicinal Chemistry and AI-Guided Ligand-Based Approach

PI: [PI NAME] | Co-I (Experimental): [CO-PI NAME] | [INSTITUTION]
Project Period: 09/01/2027 - 08/31/2030

Project Summary (public abstract):

alpha9alpha10 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChRs) are implicated in
chronic pain, tinnitus, and cochlear damage, yet no positive allosteric
modulator (PAM) of this receptor has been translated, and publicly available
nicotinic modulator chemistry (agonists/antagonists of alpha4beta2, alpha7)
does not transfer to alpha9alpha10 PAM pharmacology. Our computational
workflow has curated a validated experimental dataset of 30 compounds
(7 PAM actives), 24 of which share the L-ascorbic acid (vitamin C)
gamma-lactone enediol pharmacophore - the only validated PAM chemotype for
this target and a selective alpha9alpha10 potentiator whose L- and D-forms
are approximately equipotent. Our preliminary data show that modest
structural modifications of ascorbic acid dramatically enhance alpha9alpha10
PAM potency: substitution edits to the validated millimolar reference
(L-ascorbic acid, ID1, 1797 uM) reach a sub-micromolar 3-O-substituted,
5,6-acetonide analog (ID12, 0.198 uM, ~9,100x window) and a 6-bromo-6-deoxy
analog (ID25, 2.63 uM).     

Relevance statement (for the public):
This project uses traditional chemistry knowledge combined with computer
"design assistants" to develop and test new vitamin C-based chemical families
that safely modulate a receptor implicated in chronic pain and hearing loss,
and converts each round of results into shared, traceable chemical knowledge
that speeds up future drug discovery.

Keywords: alpha9alpha10 nAChR, positive allosteric modulators, ascorbic acid
medicinal chemistry, matched molecular pairs, closed-loop active learning,
generative AI, cheminformatics, stereochemistry, selectivity benchmarking,
retrosynthetic analysis, drug discovery

SECTION 2: SPECIFIC AIMS
=======================================================================

Title: Development of High-Potency, Selective Ascorbate-Derived alpha9alpha10
Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor (nAChR) Positive Allosteric Modulators
(PAMs) through an Integrated Medicinal Chemistry and AI-Guided Ligand-Based
Approach

alpha9alpha10 nAChRs mediate efferent innervation of the cochlea and are
implicated in chronic pain, tinnitus, and noise-induced hearing loss; genetic
deletion renders animals resistant to several pain models, making this receptor
a compelling non-opioid pain target and an otoprotection target. Therapeutic
translation requires PAMs that do not duplicate the toxicity profile of
nicotinic agonists. Our computational workflow has curated 30 validated
compounds (7 PAM actives), all sharing the ascorbate gamma-lactone enediol
pharmacophore - 24/30 carry the core SMARTS OC1=C(O)C(=O)OC1-[#6] and all 7
actives sit on it. We generated a 97-analog prospective candidate set from
the 7 confirmed actives and filtered it honestly: 86 analogs were rejected
(connected-fragment chemistry gate), leaving 15 unique molecules
(9 chemistry-validated analogs + 6 negative controls); after pre-registered
filters (PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski) only 6 are drug-like. The gap is that (i) modest structural edits to
ascorbic acid show dramatic potency gains (ID12, 0.198 uM; ID25, 2.63 uM)
whose governing structural features are not yet systematically mapped, and
(ii) no experimental round has yet established which predicted features
actually govern alpha9alpha10 potentiation. Our long-term goal is a
medicinally driven analog series of high-potency, selective alpha9alpha10
PAMs, supported by an AI-guided design platform that prioritizes and validates
candidates for any future drug-discovery or preclinical program.

CENTRAL HYPOTHESIS:
A medicinal-chemistry-driven, iterative closed loop in which position-resolved
SAR analysis from classic synthetic chemistry leads, computational/AI tools
supplement analog design, and electrophysiological evaluation closes each
round - rather than extrapolation from a small dataset - identifies the
structural features that govern alpha9alpha10 potentiation and delivers
high-potency, selective ascorbate-derived PAMs, with a predictive
computational model of alpha9alpha10 PAM activity that supports (but does not
replace) medicinal-chemistry decisions each round.

AIM 1 (single integrated aim). Develop high potency and selective
ascorbate-derived alpha9alpha10 positive allosteric modulators through an
integrated medicinal chemistry and AI-guided ligand-based approach.

Our preliminary studies demonstrate that modest structural modifications of
ascorbic acid can dramatically enhance alpha9alpha10 PAM potency. We will
combine classic medicinal chemistry, structure-activity relationship
analysis, and generative AI-guided design to identify the structural features
that govern alpha9alpha10 potentiation. Newly synthesized analogs will be
evaluated using electrophysiological assays, employing an iterative approach
to optimize the pharmacophore. These results will produce a predictive
computational model of alpha9alpha10 PAM activity to guide subsequent rounds
of analog design.

Operationally, this one aim contains four coupled activities (delivered in
parallel, not as sequential aims):

1a. Medicinal chemistry + SAR-guided analog design. Systematically edit the
    ascorbate gamma-lactone envelope around the position-resolved SAR
    derived from 30 validated compounds: 3-O-alkoxy + 5,6-acetonide is the
    high-potency arm (ID12, 0.198 uM, most potent in set); 6-bromo-6-deoxy
    (ID25, 2.63 uM) is second; 2-O-alkoxy (ID24) and 6-O-benzyl (ID18) are
    weak actives; 2,3-bis-O-alkylation ablates activity (0/6); 3-O-alkoxy
    open chain (0/3) and non-ascorbate compounds (0/6) are inactive.
    The synthetic program is organized into five integrated studies:
    Study 1.1 (role of the C-2 hydroxyl - 2-deoxy/2-halo/2-O-acyl/2-O-alkyl
    series), Study 1.2 (conformationally rigid bicyclic ascorbate analogs -
    constrained C-5/C-6 and enediol geometry), Study 1.3 (C-3-O-heterocyclic
    analogs - O-linked azoles/oxetanes/small heteroarylalkyl along the
    highest-potency vector), Study 1.4 (C-5/C-6 hydroxyl role -
    deoxygenation/olefination series, ~10 analogs), and Study 1.5
    (deoxy-ascorbate mimics and azalactams via Dutta-lactone-derived
    cyclopropane/epoxide/aziridine bicyclic lactones, ~8/category); 56
    ascorbate-restricted matched-molecular-pair transforms (the 6-bromo-6-
    deoxy edit is the largest activity cliff) guide the enumeration. All
    analogs must pass pre-registered PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski
    gates and are reported with provenance, never fabricated.

1b. Computational design support (supplements 1a, not a separate deliverable).
    REINVENT4 Mol2Mol with a stereo-aware prior and an openly
    documented rank score (predicted activity x information-gain boost,
    scaffold-capped) is the planned in-loop generator; consensus
    explanations flag which proposed edit is expected to drive potency;
    planned synthetic probes are prioritized by route-prediction decision
    support (phase 4c). Responsibly scoped: REINVENT4 generates NO molecules
    (binary absent; recorded READY_PENDING_REINVENT_BINARY, never fabricated),
    so the current prospective set derives from the traditional
    medicinal-chemistry baseline (components pool via study 1a, 97 enumerated
    / 86 chemistry-invalid / 11 valid; 9 carried into the candidate set plus 6
    negative controls). Once installed, generator output will stream through
    the same pre-registered filters and be reported as prospective; the
    computational proposals feed - they do not replace - the
    medicinal-chemistry analog plan.

1c. Iterative electrophysiological evaluation. Each round tests ~15 drug-like
    analogs (round 1 = the 6 eligible) split into a model-ranked arm and an equal-size
    diversity-randomized control arm (matched on chemotype tier), all blinded
    (R{n}-{crc32} IDs), by two-electrode voltage-clamp on alpha9alpha10-
    expressing Xenopus oocytes (co-PI laboratory), >=3 oocytes per compound,
    blind operator to arm. Primary endpoint: potentiation>0 AND potency>0,
    confirmed by independent dose-response. Pre-registered before any data:
    enrichment factor (threshold 2.0), Benjamini-Hochberg FDR (alpha=0.10),
    precision@k, arm-stratified hit rates, go/no-go (continue iff EF>=2.0 and
    >=1 clean novel chemotype confirmed; max 3 rounds). Oversight gate:
    medicinal-chemistry sign-off on synthesizability (supported by phase 4c
    route plans), stereo feasibility, assay suitability, TAF consistency, and
    negative-control balance; no experiment proceeds before approval.

1d. Predictive model construction and iterative refit. After each round,
    append results (never overwrite), refit the predictive model (RandomForest
    200 trees, Morgan r2 2048 + 10 descriptors, StandardScaler) with the LAST
    round held out, versioned as models/classical/lbm_rf_fp_desc_rN.joblib;
    update TAF evidence and power from pooled variability (explicit
    prohibition on post-hoc re-powering). Deliver at least 3 rounds / >=3
    enriched-SAR releases (or a formal failure report if EF stays below
    threshold), culminating in a validated predictive model of alpha9alpha10
    PAM activity that guides all subsequent analog design.

Selectivity and synthesizability are enforced inside the loop, not as
separate aims: before plate locking, each analog's frozen-model selectivity
score across the 70-compound non-alpha9alpha10 panel (phase 4b) and its
route-prediction plan + building-block stock status (phase 4c) are re-checked
and reported in the pre-registration file.

Impact:
First experimentally enumerated, high-potency and selective ascorbate-derived
alpha9alpha10 PAM series; a predictive, validated model of alpha9alpha10 PAM
activity; a selectivity benchmark making off-target cross-subtype activity an
explicit, measured design axis; route-prediction decision support making
synthesizability reproducible; and an audit trail that converts
machine-learning predictions into chemically validated, reusable knowledge.

SECTION 3: RESEARCH STRATEGY
=======================================================================

3.1 SIGNIFICANCE

Disease relevance. alpha9alpha10 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors mediate
efferent innervation of the cochlea and are implicated in chronic pain,
tinnitus, and noise-induced hearing loss. Genetic deletion renders animals
resistant to several pain models, making this receptor a compelling non-opioid
pain target and an otoprotection target. Therapeutic translation requires
positive allosteric modulators (PAMs) that do not duplicate the toxicity
profile of nicotinic agonists.

Chemical gap. Known nicotinic modulators (agonists/antagonists of alpha4beta2,
alpha7) are based on amine and quaternary chemistries that do not transfer to
alpha9alpha10 PAM pharmacology. There is no experimentally enumerated PAM
chemotype library. Our preliminary data: a curated internal dataset of 30
validated compounds yields 7 PAM actives spanning 17 scaffolds/series; 24/30
share the ascorbate gamma-lactone enediol core, and all 7 actives sit on it.
A computational panel reaches leave-one-compound-out balanced accuracy 0.77
(ridge descriptors) and scaffold/series-out balanced accuracy 0.80 (gradient
boosting with fingerprints + descriptors). These metrics are honest but
modest - exactly the situation that motivates empirical analog optimization
rather than extrapolation.

Opportunity. Modest structural edits to a validated millimolar reference set
potency: the 3-O-propargyl-5,6-acetonide edit (ID12, 0.198 uM) and the
6-bromo-6-deoxy edit (ID25, 2.63 uM) are ~9,100-fold and ~680-fold more
potent than L-ascorbic acid (ID1, 1797 uM) while retaining potentiation.
This demonstrates that substitution edits, not the enediol core itself, set
activity, and brings a validated vitamin-C pharmacophore into the
drug-relevant sub-micromolar window. The integrated medicinal-chemistry +
AI plan is designed to systematically mine that potency headroom and to make
selectivity (cross-subtype) and synthesizability explicit, measured design
axes.

What is missing (and is addressed here).
(1) A systematic map of which structural features govern alpha9alpha10
potentiation: no experimental round has yet been run; the closed-loop
mechanism (comparator arm, pre-registration, oversight gate, append-only
refits) is implemented and verified end-to-end but is a plan to execute in
this award, framed honestly as such. (2) High-potency, selective analogs: the
current candidate portfolio (32 hypothesis-group assignments over 15 unique
molecules = 9 chemistry-validated analogs + 6 negative controls; groups
A=1, B=6, C=1, D=5, E=12, F=7 assignments) contains only 6 unique drug-like
synthetically-credible molecules after pre-registered
PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski filtering and deduplication - a finding the field needs
to see, and the starting point for the med-chem/generative fill-in this aim
executes. (3) A quantified selectivity baseline: the 70-compound
non-alpha9alpha10 panel provides the first measured cross-subtype response
surface of the model (mean predicted alpha9alpha10 activity 0.215 +/- 0.045;
0/70 above the 0.5 threshold) and makes selectivity designable rather than
assumed. (4) Reproducible synthesizability: route-prediction decision support
makes the oversight gate's synthesizability review concrete.

3.2 INNOVATION

1. Medicinal-chemistry-led, AI-supplemented loop. The loop is driven by classic
   med-chem edits (MMP-guided, position-resolved SAR); generative AI (REINVENT4
   Mol2Mol, stereo-aware prior) supplements the medicinal-chemistry plan by
   scoring the SAME rank metric (predicted activity x information-gain,
   scaffold-capped) to prioritize which planned analogs to make and to flag
   competing hypotheses - computational support in service of the
   medicinal-chemistry program, not a parallel deliverable.
2. Honesty as method. Constant/uninformative explanation methods are flagged
   rather than promoted; non-significant permutation nulls are reported;
   the last experimental round is held out of every refit; power is recomputed
   from pooled variability with an explicit prohibition on post-hoc
   re-powering; overclaim-scan flags unsupported language automatically;
   generative/AI outputs with no molecules (e.g., absent REINVENT4 binary)
   are reported with provenance, not fabricated.
3. Drug-discovery-ready filtering inside the loop. PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski gates
   and scaffold caps run inside plate design, converting a raw virtual
   portfolio into a curated analog series and reporting the realistic 6/15
   eligible set instead of overclaiming throughput.
4. Stereochemistry made testable. Enantiomer/diastereomer probes are
   engineered into plates (22 designed from the 7 actives) so stereo
   hypotheses (e.g., the L/D-tolerant ascorbate pharmacophore) become
   experimentally distinguishable rather than 2D counterfactuals only.
5. Selectivity as a measured design axis (phase 4b). A curated, isolated panel
   of 70 non-alpha9alpha10 nAChR modulators with an enforced training-isolation
   contract is scored by the frozen model at design time; cross-subtype
   potential becomes an explicit, re-evaluated filter inside the loop instead
   of an afterthought.
6. Route-prediction decision support (phase 4c). Retrosynthetic route planning
   (AIZynthFinder, public USPTO templates + ZINC stock) is a live pipeline
   phase that appends PREDICTED_ROUTE/UNVERIFIED plans and a per-building-block
   stock screen to every plate candidate, giving the oversight gate's
   synthesizability review concrete, reproducible, honest decision support
   (including explicit NO_ROUTE_FOUND reporting) instead of a hand-waved
   assumption.
7. Dual focus: systematic ascorbate SAR + cochlear-delivery-compatible design.
   The aim is innovative in its dual focus: (1) systematic SAR of ascorbate
   to define structural requirements for alpha9alpha10 PAM activity, and
   (2) strategic design of transporter-compatible analogs to facilitate
   targeted cochlear delivery. The use of advanced synthetic methodologies
   (deoxygenation, olefination, azalactam and rigid bicyclic construction)
   combined with mechanistic design enhances the potential for developing
   dual-acting compounds that are both potent PAMs and deliverable to the
   cochlea. Because we have already succeeded in identifying a significantly
   more potent analog of ascorbic acid (ID12, 0.198 uM; ID25, 2.63 uM), the
   success of this aim appears likely - and is tested empirically in the
   pre-registered rounds.

3.3 APPROACH

3.3.1 Preliminary data (all verified in this repository; 19-phase pipeline
runs end-to-end, all phases pass).

Dataset governance. 30 compounds; 7 actives (IDs 1,2,3,12,18,24,25); 23
tautomers normalized; stereochemistry audit completed; append-only pooling with
leakage audit and an 18-question final audit at every pipeline pass.

Ascorbate SAR sub-series (novel finding). Reference L-ascorbic acid (ID1,
1797 uM, 286%) and its D-form isoascorbate (ID2, 1316 uM, 300%; PI-note L/D
assignment - stereo undefined in source) both potentiate with similar potency,
i.e., ascorbate is a stereochemically tolerant pharmacophore and a selective
alpha9alpha10 potentiator (PI note; not yet computationally modeled).
Position-resolved annotation (standard ascorbate numbering):
  * 3-O-alkoxy, 5,6-acetonide: 8 compounds (IDs 5-12), 1 active (ID12),
    0.198 uM = MOST POTENT in the set (~9,100x below the 1797 uM reference;
    PI shorthand "3-O-ethyl" arm)
  * 6-bromo-6-deoxy: 1 compound (ID25), 1 active, 2.63 uM (second most
    potent; ~680x gain over reference L-ascorbate)
  * 6-O-benzyl ether: 1 compound (ID18), 1 active, 1288 uM
  * 2-O-alkoxy: 1 compound (ID24), 1 active, 1202 uM
  * Free ascorbate: 3 compounds (IDs 1, 2, 4), 2 actives (66.7% - the two
    reference stereoisomers)
  * 5,6-acetonide, free enediol: 1 compound (ID3), 1 active, 6077 uM
  * 2,3-bis-O-alkoxy (open or acetonide): 6 compounds (IDs 22, 13-17),
    0 actives; 3-O-alkoxy open chain: 3 compounds (IDs 19-21), 0 actives
  * Non-ascorbate: 6 compounds (IDs 23, 26-30), 0 actives
Pharmacophore envelope: 3-O-alkylation is compatible with activity and, with
a small 3-O-substituent plus 5,6-acetonide, defines the most potent compound;
2-O-alkylation is tolerated but weak; 2,3-bis-O-alkylation ablates activity;
C6 halogenation (6-bromo-6-deoxy) delivers the sharpest single-edit potency
gain over the reference. Potency headroom: the validated reference is
millimolar (1797 uM), yet sub-series edits reach 0.198 uM and 2.63 uM -
i.e., substitution edits, not the enediol core, set potency, bringing a
validated vitamin-C pharmacophore into the drug-relevant sub-micromolar
window. 56 ascorbate-restricted MMP transforms (both arms on core) were
computed; the 6-bromo-6-deoxy edit is the largest activity cliff and guides
C6-halogen focus.

PI stereoelectronic SAR hypotheses (recorded verbatim; PI notes, validated
against the 30-compound set where possible).
(a) Enolic hydroxyls required for potentiation - PARTIAL support: 2,3-bis-O-
alkylation ablates activity (0/6), but mono-etherification is tolerated
(2-O compound 24 active; 3-O compound 12 active and most potent), so a free
enol-OH per se is not required.
(b) C2=C3 olefin essential - UNTESTED: no saturation probe exists in the
30-compound set; inferred from the enediol requirement (flagged as a
hypothesis to be probed).
(c) C-3 etherification increases potency - supported ONLY for compound 12
(3-O-propargyl-5,6-acetonide, 0.198 uM); 7/8 3-O-alkoxy acetonide analogs
(compounds 5-11) and all open-chain 3-O-alkoxy (compounds 19-21) are
inactive, so "alkyl substituents well tolerated" overstates the SAR.
(d) C-6 substitution decreases potency - NOT SUPPORTED: 6-bromo-6-deoxy
(compound 25) is 2.63 uM (~680x gain over reference) and 6-O-benzyl
(compound 18, 1288 uM) is comparable to the reference; no C-6 substitution
in the dataset reduces potency.
Ryanodine analogy (PI hypothesis, speculative): ascorbic acid shares
structural and functional similarities with the polyhydroxy cyclic moieties
of ryanodine; proposed roles for the enediol in metal ion-dipole/chelation
and hydrogen bonding. Framing only; not testable from the current dataset.

Model validation. LOCO best balanced accuracy 0.77 (ridge descriptors; RF
fingerprints+descriptors 0.867 accuracy / 0.714 balanced); scaffold-out 0.80
(GBM fingerprints+descriptors; RF 0.767); series-out 0.80 (GBM; RF 0.767).
Bootstrap confidence intervals computed; permutation nulls not significant -
feature importances are treated as corroborative only, never as mechanistic
proof.

Selectivity panel (phase 4b, NEW). Curated 70 unique non-alpha9alpha10 nAChR
modulators from 74 raw source entries (4 canonical-SMILES duplicates removed:
Compound9, CMPI-2b, Struc8/Struc11, TBS-156/TBS-516). Sources span alpha7 PAMs
(PNU-120596, A-867744, NS-1738, TQS, PAM-2, PAM-4), alpha4beta2 PAM NS-9283,
the alpha3alpha5beta2 (barbiturate) series, indole/benzamide/urea and
flavonoid chemotypes (quercetin, genistein, 5-HI, isoliquiritigenin). Isolation
contract enforced: 0 exact-SMILES and 0 scaffold overlap with the alpha9alpha10
training set; panel stored under selectivity/ only and never merged into
training labels. Frozen model benchmark: mean predicted alpha9alpha10 activity
0.215 +/- 0.045; 0/70 above the 0.5 threshold (alpha9alpha10 actives, by
contrast, score >=0.64 with mean 0.797). This is the first quantified
selectivity baseline for the model and is re-evaluated inside the loop as a
design filter.

Explanation stability. Across-model/seed stability STABLE (Spearman 0.88-0.89)
but cross-method agreement UNSTABLE (~0.2) - motivates consensus fusion plus
explicit flagging rather than any single explanation method. TAF evidence
matrix: TAF-3 only MODERATE (3 corroborative in-silico sources); TAF-1/2/4/6
LIMITED.

Library projection. 8 Bemis-Murcko-distinct chemotype families (tiers: 2
tier-2 hypothesis-group families, 6 tier-3 residual; 15 molecules = 8
tier-2 + 7 tier-3); 7 novel scaffolds vs training; after PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski +

Closed-loop phase (implemented and verified). Consensus explanations, stereo

Route-prediction decision support (phase 4c, NEW). Retrosynthetic route

round's plate
via a route_prediction column. Runs in an isolated Python 3.12 venv

Structural models. AF3 binding model in validation phase (co-PI laboratory);

3.3.2 Aim 1 - Medicinal-chemistry-driven analog program with computational/AI
support.

Design. Take the 32-assignment candidate set (15 unique = 9 chemistry-validated
analogs + 6 negative controls; groups A-F assignments 1/6/1/5/12/7) and the
30-compound validated SAR. Apply, in order: (i) deduplicate by
molecule; (ii) largest-fragment canonical SMILES + Bemis-Murcko scaffold
assignment; (iii) PAINS filter, BRENK reactive-substructure filter, Lipinski
gate (MW<=500, cLogP<=5, HBD<=5, HBA<=10, <=2 violations); (iv) tier
assignment (1 = novel scaffold + clean; 2 = hypothesis-group member; 3 =
residual); (v) scaffold cap of 2 per family per plate.

Deliverables. chemotype_families.csv (families, tiers, representative,
priority), chemotype_library_annotated.csv, coverage_targets.csv
(pre-registered per-round new-chemotype targets, EF threshold, go/no-go),
stereo probe set (22 probes from 7 actives), ascorbate sub-series SAR cards,
and per-round route + stock annotations (phase 4c).

Analog generation (medicinal-chemistry-driven; AI as supplement). The
medicinal-chemistry analog program is organized into five integrated synthesis
studies (1.1-1.5), each targeting a defined structural feature of the
ascorbate pharmacophore; computational tools (predictive model, generative AI
proposals, route-prediction support) help prioritize which planned analogs to
advance but do not drive the program; all compounds pass pre-registered
PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski gates and are reported with provenance, never fabricated.

STUDY 1.1. Role of the C-2 hydroxyl group in potentiation of alpha9alpha10
nAChRs. The dataset shows C-2 mono-alkylation is tolerated but weak (ID24,
2-O-alkoxy, 1202 uM) whereas 2,3-bis-O-alkylation ablates activity (0/6),
implying the C-2 OH is dispensable only when the C-3 enediol environment is
intact. We will synthesize a C-2 edit series - 2-deoxy-2-halo (F, Cl, Br),
2-O-acyl, and 2-O-alkyl analogs preserving the C-3 OH - plus C-2 epimeric
probes, to map whether C-2 substitution is tolerated, enhances, or blocks
potentiation. C-2 is the minimal-edit locus closest to the core, so it
directly tests the enol-OH dependence hypothesis (partial support from the
dataset) with the smallest structural perturbation.

STUDY 1.2. Synthesis of conformationally rigid bicyclic ascorbic acid
analogs. Constrain the C-5/C-6 side chain and enediol geometry into fused
bicyclic frameworks (5,6-acetonide and cyclopentano/oxabicyclic locks around
the gamma-lactone) to pre-organize the pharmacophore in its presumed
active conformation. The 5,6-acetonide (ID3, free enediol, 6077 uM) and the
most-potent 3-O-substituted 5,6-acetonide (ID12, 0.198 uM) bracket the rigid
5,6-lock's effect; the study will decouple side-chain rigidity from
substitution at C-3 and test whether enforced conformation raises potency or
selectivity across the series.

STUDY 1.3. Design and synthesis of C-3-O-heterocyclic ascorbic acid analogs.
The C-3-O position is the highest-potency vector in the dataset (ID12,
3-O-propargyl-5,6-acetonide, 0.198 uM) but 7/8 other C-3-O-alkoxy acetonides
are inactive, so the SAR at C-3 is narrow and needs systematic mapping. We
will synthesize C-3-O-heterocyclic analogs - O-linked azole/azine/oxetane and
small heteroarylalkyl substituents (triazoles, pyridylmethyl, oxetanylmethyl,
tetrahydrofuranylmethyl), plus heteroatom-containing linkers - to define the
exact steric/electronic envelope that converts the C-3 vector from
essentially binary (1/8 active) to a reliable potency handle.

STUDY 1.4. Probing the role of C-5 and C-6 hydroxyl groups in ascorbic acid
analogs. The only C-5/C-6 blockade in the dataset (5,6-acetonide, ID3,
6077 uM) is ~3.4x less potent than the reference, yet the single C-6 edit
(6-bromo-6-deoxy, ID25, 2.63 uM) is ~680x more potent - a C-5/C-6 paradox
that we resolve by deoxygenation/olefination: a fully deoxygenated C-5/C-6
analog (Barton-McCombie) and a dehydrated C-5/C-6 olefinic handle
(Ru-catalyzed cross-metathesis) afford rigid steric/electronic probes with
varied R5 (1d series, ~10 analogs). Predictions are compound-specific, never
blanket.

STUDY 1.5. Probe the significance of deoxy-ascorbate mimics and azalactams.
Replace the enediol/diol periphery with nitrogen: L-serine-derived chiral
amino-butenolides (Dutta lactone) cyclize to azacarbohydrates, and the
unsaturated lactone is elaborated by Pd-cyclopropanation, in-situ
epoxidation, or aziridination to bicyclic lactams/azalactams (~8/category)
that mimic ascorbate with distinct stereoelectronics (O replaced/modulated by
CH2 or N). These probe the C2=C3 enediol requirement (currently UNTESTED in
the dataset) and deliver the aza/deoxy chemotypes flagged as de-prioritized
by brute force but potentially functionally relevant to this target.

(b) REINVENT4 Mol2Mol generation (stereo-aware prior): currently NO molecules
exist because the binary is absent; any generated molecules must pass the same
filters and are reported with provenance, not fabricated.
Both feed the same rank metric (predicted activity x information-gain boost,
scaffold-capped) and the same oversight gate; synthesis studies 1.1-1.5 are
prioritized (and rounds loaded) using that rank metric combined with the
phase-4c route plans.

Selectivity filter inside the loop. Before plate locking, the frozen model's
selectivity panel score (mean predicted alpha9alpha10 activity across 70
non-alpha9alpha10 modulators) and each candidate's structural overlap with
panel scaffolds are re-evaluated and reported in the pre-registration file, so
cross-subtype potential is a documented, re-checked design axis (phase 4b).

Synthesizability filter inside the loop. Before plate locking, each
candidate's retrosynthetic route plan (PREDICTED_ROUTE/UNVERIFIED, phase 4c)
and building-block availability (IN_STOCK/NOT_IN_STOCK) are attached to the
plate record, so the oversight gate's synthesizability review reads
reproducible evidence rather than assumptions. NO_ROUTE_FOUND candidates are
explicitly flagged for the medicinal chemist to route manually - never
silently claimed.

3.3.3 Iterative closed-loop analog rounds.

Design. Per round: ~15 drug-like analogs (or the full eligible set if
smaller; round 1 = 6). Arms: model_ranked (top by rank score = predicted
activity x information-gain boost, scaffold-capped) vs diversity_random
(scaffold-capped random control). All blinded (R{n}-{crc32} IDs). Oversight
gate: medicinal-chemistry sign-off on synthesizability (supported by phase 4c
route plans), stereo feasibility, assay suitability, TAF consistency, and
negative-control balance; no experiment proceeds before approval.
Pre-registered prior to data: primary endpoint, EF threshold 2.0, BH-FDR
alpha 0.10, confirmation by independent dose-response, go/no-go (continue iff
EF>=2.0 and >=1 clean novel chemotype confirmed; max 3 rounds).

Methods. Two-electrode voltage-clamp on commercially sourced Xenopus oocytes;
concentration-response for potentiation on alpha9alpha10. Each compound >=3
oocytes, blind operator to arm. Activity = potentiation>0 AND potency>0,
confirmed by independent dose-response.

Analysis (pre-registered). EF_topk = (hits_topk/k)/(hits_total/n) per arm;
arm-stratified hit rates; precision@k; BH-FDR across compounds when replicate
p-values are available; enrichment factor vs pre-registered threshold.

Model update after each round. Append-only pooling (never
duplicate/overwrite); refit RandomForest (200 trees, Morgan r2 2048 + 10
descriptors, StandardScaler) with the LAST round held out; versioned artifact
models/classical/lbm_rf_fp_desc_rN.joblib; power recomputed from pooled
variability (no post-hoc re-powering). TAF evidence updated only from confirmed
hits, with source counts tracked.

Power/caveat. With ~9-15 per round, individual-round statistics are weak; the
statistical unit is the pooled comparison across all rounds, which is why EF is
evaluated on pooled top-k and why the primary readout is the comparator-arm
contrast, not per-compound p-values. If EF is below threshold in 3 rounds, the
go/no-go fires a failure report - a publishable negative that prevents
overfitting to a small dataset.

3.3.4 Selectivity as a design axis (phase 4b) - implementation.

A dedicated pipeline phase (after model training, before plate design)
controls the otherModulators panel and its benchmark:
  - Curation: discover sources (repo-root CSV + data/selectivity/raw/*.csv
    drop-ins + config override), canonical-SMILES dedup (first-seen source
    wins), full cheminformatics annotation with the project descriptor set,
    Murcko scaffold extraction, PAINS/BRENK/Lipinski summary.
  - Isolation contract: exact-SMILES overlap with the alpha9alpha10 training
    set must equal 0; panel is stored under selectivity/ only and is never
    written to data/processed; the training CSV is untouched. Guards run at
    every pipeline pass and the result is hashed into the manifest.
  - Benchmark: frozen model scoring (RF, Morgan r2 2048 + 10 descriptors) on
    the panel; scaffold overlap with training set and with the candidate
    chemotype families; report threshold exceedances (>=0.5).
  - Expandability: future sources are drop-in CSVs in data/selectivity/raw/
    with an on-disk README schema; each source is sha256-hashed and logged, so
    the panel grows without code changes and without breaking isolation.
Current results: 70 compounds, 0 exact-SMILES and 0 scaffold overlaps, mean
predicted activity 0.215 +/- 0.045, 0/70 >= 0.5. The panel is versioned in the
season-2026 selectivity manifest.

3.3.5 Route-prediction decision support (phase 4c) - implementation.

Retrosynthetic route planning (AIZynthFinder, public USPTO expansion/reaction
templates + ZINC building-block stock) is AVAILABLE as a live phase that
feeds the oversight gate's synthesizability review rather than replacing the
medicinal chemist. It runs in an isolated Python 3.12 venv (aizynthfinder
4.4.1) against locally downloaded USPTO model/template files and the ZINC
stock; the phase honestly self-reports NOT AVAILABLE (no routes written) if
the models or venv are absent, so nothing is fabricated. Current status:
  - 8 round-1 targets evaluated; 1 PREDICTED_ROUTE plan retained (1-step,
    with the route product verified against the target via substructure
    match), 7 NO_ROUTE_FOUND reported with recorded probabilities rather
    than blank claims.
  - Plans are labeled PREDICTED_ROUTE / UNVERIFIED - decision support only,
    never a synthesis guarantee; the med-chem oversight gate remains the
    binding authority on synthesizability and stereo feasibility.
  - Building-block coverage report: 38 unique blocks across the attempted
    routes, 18 marked IN_STOCK (ZINC) and 20 NOT_IN_STOCK - a concrete,
    per-block stock screen
    for round-1 procurement, versioned under synth_route/.
  - Loop integration: the round plate and oversight-gate CSV carry a
    route_prediction column that links each candidate to its route plan, so
    synthesizability is re-checked at design time every round.
Deliverable: AI-ready route + stock annotations appended to candidate records,
traceable to model files and seeds.

3.3.6 Power and statistics.

All statistics are pre-registered per round (section 3.3.3). Enrichment factor
and FDR are evaluated on pooled data across rounds; power is recomputed from
pooled variability with no post-hoc re-powering. LOCO/scaffold-out/series-out
metrics in the Preliminary Data are retrospective internal fits reported with
CIs and non-significant permutation nulls as honesty flags.

3.3.7 Pitfalls and alternatives.

Covered inline above: over-filtering to zero molecules (SAR-probe tier);
low eligible pool (adaptive round size, already implemented); EF<2.0 in 3
rounds (formal failure report, publishable); external data heterogeneity
(harmonization report; internal holdout as primary guard); explanation
instability (consensus fusion + flagging); stereo ambiguity in the L/D
reference assignment (PI note; stereo probes make it testable); PAINS/BRENK on
functionally relevant series (med-chem review); route planner coverage gaps
(NO_ROUTE_FOUND targets reported with probabilities, then routed by the
medicinal chemist, not silently claimed); generative-AI absence (REINVENT4
binary absent -> reported as prospective, never fabricated; med-chem
enumeration carries the analog expansion until the generator is available);
synthesis difficulty in the constrained rigid series (Studies 1.2/1.5) - if a
planned bicyclic/azalactam route fails, the analog is replaced by a
synthetically tractable scaffold-matched alternative and the substitution is
logged at the oversight gate, never silently dropped.
